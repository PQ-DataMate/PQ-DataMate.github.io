Case study 03 · Business Analysis & Decision Support

Sales Performance & PTA Reporting

A monthly Excel report connecting assigned sales targets with actual customer revenue — from individual sales representatives to the national level.

CRM exports Excel reporting KPI analysis
My role: I received the approved target values and prepared monthly performance reporting. Budget setting and target allocation were handled by sales management.
01 / Process

How it worked

CRM data

Exported customer turnover and CRM ownership data to attribute each customer’s results to the assigned sales representative.

Monthly comparison

Compared actual turnover against the provided targets and calculated each sales representative’s percentage of target achievement.

Sales representative → Branch → Region → Poland
02 / Reporting scope

Metrics adapted to annual business rules

Depending on the year’s targets, reporting covered total turnover, turnover by customer product, new sales, existing sales and upselling.

Target achievement % Customer revenue Product-level turnover New & existing sales Upselling
03 / Business value

A consistent view of sales performance

The monthly report helped sales management track target achievement at individual and aggregated levels, using a consistent view of customer turnover and sales ownership.
