02 / Oracle Sales Cloud

Reporting built from the ground up.

Independently developed reports, dashboards and automated reporting workflows without formal training or dedicated IT support. I learned through testing, troubleshooting and iterating until the solutions worked as intended.

Sales pipeline & Hit Rate

Reporting on open and closed opportunities, sales stages, pipeline value and Hit Rate - a key performance indicator for the Sales Division, measuring the share of won opportunities among all closed opportunities.

Sales activity reporting

Visibility into sales activities, including visits, phone calls, quotations and other customer-related interactions.

Automated notifications

Configured reporting agents and conditions, including notifications to opportunity owners when opportunities became overdue.

Scheduled reporting

Set up Friday end-of-week reports for sales users and Monday morning reporting for managers preparing for weekly board meetings.

04 / Internal BI · TMS & SAP

From shipment-level detail to customer profitability.

Independently built detailed reports using internal BI data sourced from a Transport Management System (TMS) and SAP financial data. The reporting connected operational shipment records with financial information, then aggregated the details into customer-level views for selected reporting periods.

01. Shipment-level data Individual shipments identified by shipment or consignment number.
02. Operational & financial analysis Transport details, revenue, surcharges, production costs and gross profit.
03. Customer-level insight Aggregated performance by customer, quarter or year to date.

Shipment-level detail

Collection and delivery locations, dates, shipment identifiers, goods descriptions, dimensions, weight, volume, pallets and packaging information.

Revenue and cost components

Transport rates, fuel adjustments, seasonal supplements, toll charges, production costs, gross profit and margin.

Service quality

On-time indicators, delays, claims and selected additional services such as fixed-day delivery or special handling.

Customer-level aggregation

Combining shipment records into a consolidated view of customer performance over a quarter or year-to-date period, supporting commercial analysis and customer discussions.

Much of my reporting experience was developed through hands-on problem-solving. I built reports from scratch, tested different approaches, refined report logic and resolved issues until the solutions worked as intended.

I focused on making data useful in practice: helping sales users monitor their pipeline, giving managers a consistent reporting view and enabling customer performance to be analysed from shipment-level operational and financial data.
