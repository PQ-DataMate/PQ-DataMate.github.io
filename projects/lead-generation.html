Accelerating Sales with
Lead Generation

From external company data to qualified sales leads and a measurable pipeline.

Lead generation Data preparation CRM Sales operations SharePoint Process design Reporting & dashboards
01 / The Challenge

Turn external company data into real sales opportunities

The business objective was to systematically reach out to new companies in Poland, identify potential transport needs and build an additional sales pipeline. External databases contained company and contact details, business activity classifications and other firmographic information, but required preparation, segmentation and a clear process before they could be used by Sales Support.

◎ Key goals
  • ◉Use external company data effectively for outbound prospecting
  • ♟Enable Sales Support with a structured, easy-to-follow process
  • ▥Give management visibility into activity, results and pipeline development
02 / My Role

Designed and implemented the operational solution

The initiative was defined by senior management. I was responsible for turning it into a practical, repeatable workflow - from preparing the data to enabling users and building management reporting.

  • Prepared and structured external company records
  • Matched records with internal customer data to exclude existing customers
  • Selected relevant business activities and assigned regions and branches
  • Published the prepared database on SharePoint
  • Defined CRM lead creation, handover and opportunity workflow
  • Created dashboards, instructions and user training

Custom reporting for different audiences
