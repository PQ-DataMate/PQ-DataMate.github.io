Accelerating Sales with Lead Generation

Building a structured prospecting process that turned external company data into actionable sales leads, CRM opportunities and management visibility.

Lead Generation Data Preparation CRM SharePoint Pipeline Reporting
01 / Business Challenge

From raw company records to a structured sales process.

The business initiative was to identify potential customers in Poland and support outbound sales activities through targeted cold calling. External company databases contained company details, contact information, business activity classifications and other firmographic information.

My responsibility was to turn this raw input into a usable, structured process for Sales Support and the sales organisation, while avoiding unnecessary contact with existing customers and giving management visibility into progress.

02 / My Contribution

Designed and implemented the operational workflow.

Data preparation

Cleaned and structured external company records, matched them against internal customer information, excluded existing customers and segmented prospects by business activity, region and branch.

Process design

Defined the workflow for prospect review, cold calling, qualification, CRM lead creation and handover to the responsible salesperson.

Tools and documentation

Published the prepared database on SharePoint and created process instructions and supporting materials for users.

Training and reporting

Trained users and developed management dashboards to track database processing, leads created, opportunity development and the resulting sales pipeline.

The initial business initiative was established by senior management. My contribution focused on data preparation, operational process design, implementation, user enablement and reporting.
03 / Process Design

A clear path from prospect to opportunity.

The workflow connected prospecting activity with the existing CRM process, making lead handover and follow-up more structured.

STEP 01

Prepare and qualify

Enrich, segment and filter external company records using internal customer information and business criteria.

STEP 02

Prospect and qualify interest

Sales Support contacts selected companies to understand their transport needs and potential interest.

STEP 03

Create and hand over leads

Qualified prospects are recorded as CRM Sales Leads and assigned to the appropriate salesperson for follow-up.

STEP 04

Develop the pipeline

The salesperson validates the opportunity and converts eligible leads into Opportunities for pipeline tracking.

05 / Business Value

Making prospecting measurable and repeatable.

  • Created a structured way to prepare and distribute external prospect data.
  • Reduced unnecessary prospecting against known customers through internal data matching.
  • Connected Sales Support activity with CRM lead handover and opportunity development.
  • Provided management with visibility into processing progress and lead outcomes.
  • Established supporting documentation and training for consistent execution.

The project demonstrates how data preparation, process design, CRM workflows and reporting can be combined to support a repeatable sales prospecting operation.
