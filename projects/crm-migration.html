02 / CRM Migration & User Onboarding

Helping users navigate change.

Supporting the transition from Oracle Sales Cloud to Microsoft Dynamics 365 through structured online training, user feedback and practical follow-up support.

September 2026 12 training sessions Microsoft Teams

01 / Context

The challenge

Moving to a new CRM meant adapting to a different environment, navigation and ways of working. Users needed practical guidance on everyday tasks and the differences between the old and new systems.

Understand user needs

Gather feedback through surveys and team chats to understand participants' experiences, questions and areas where they needed additional guidance.

Support the transition

Deliver topic-based training sessions, explain key CRM workflows and provide follow-up when a question required further verification.

02 / At a glance

The work in numbers.

A structured training programme supported by direct communication with users.

12 Online training sessions delivered in September 2026
4 Main training topics organised around user workflows
24 Polish responses included in the post-go-live survey analysis

04 / Training programme

Focused on everyday CRM tasks.

The sessions were divided into four practical areas, covering core navigation as well as specific sales workflows.

TOPIC 01

CRM fundamentals

Login, screens, navigation, search, customer records, activities and key differences between Oracle Sales Cloud and Dynamics 365.

TOPIC 02

Opportunities

Finding and creating opportunities, understanding sales stages and navigating the sales pipeline.

TOPIC 03

Leads

Understanding the automated lead process, searching for existing leads and creating new records.

TOPIC 04

Management reporting

Finding management reports, an introduction to CPR and a dedicated question-and-answer session.

How to interpret these results
The survey captured respondents' overall experience with Dynamics approximately one month after go-live. It was not a direct evaluation of the training sessions, and the results should not be interpreted as proof that the training alone caused these outcomes. Positive support feedback includes respondents who rated the support positively and those who had not needed support. Percentages are rounded to the nearest whole number.

07 / Key takeaway

Supporting the human side of system change.

This work combined user feedback, structured knowledge sharing and individual follow-up. The focus was on helping people understand the new CRM and giving them a clear route to ask questions when they needed further support.
